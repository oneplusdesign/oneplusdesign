RESEARCH PROBLEM01

CONSTRAINED MULTI-OBJECTIVE OPTIMIZATION

Can agents discover
Pareto improvements?

Given n stakeholders, utility functions u₁…uₙ, a feasible decision set X, constraints C, and baseline x₀, identify outcomes satisfying all constraints while improving at least one stakeholder's utility without reducing any other stakeholder's utility.

This is a standard Pareto-improvement criterion, not an original theorem. A feasible improvement may not exist. The research challenge is whether a candidate computational search mechanism outperforms established baselines under matched evaluation budgets.

Research target: reproducible multi-stakeholder mutual-gain discovery, not unverifiable welfare claims.

EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN

Benchmarks.
Not slogans.

Testable methodology and explicit limits. Conventional multi-objective optimization methods must be evaluated before any novel performance claim.

Fixed seeds, preregistered thresholds, held-out cases and published negative results.
