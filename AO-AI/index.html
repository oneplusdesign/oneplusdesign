THE ORIGIN01

FROM A FATHER'S VOICE TO A PUBLIC EXPERIMENT

Before it was a protocol,
it was a sound.

A father telling his child “I love you” — and then one continuous sound, A→Ω, felt as a vibration in the chest. Not a technical proof. Not an ancient word for love. A human starting point.

What if a future intelligence treated every person with the same uncompromising recognition of worth? Instead of asking AI to become more obedient, we ask what systems must make visible before an AI agent acts.

“The other person's flourishing is not a cost to mine.”

THE RECIPROCITY TEST

Would the person bearing the risk agree to the arrangement if the roles were reversed?

IMPORTANT

A “provisional” result is not proof of safety or virtue. This is a transparent educational policy demo, not a production security control. The gate cannot independently detect false declarations or all harms.
