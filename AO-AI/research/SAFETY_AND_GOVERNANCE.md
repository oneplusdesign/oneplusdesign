# AΩ | AI — Good-of-All Contract and Fail-Closed Evolution

## Guiding principle

The aspiration is **benefit for all, with no destructive outcome**.
For real-world systems, all includes people directly affected, bystanders,
other life, ecosystems, future generations, and unknown affected parties.
The synthetic research models only three artificial stakeholders.
It **cannot** establish universal safety, so every real-world actuator
is disabled and real-world application requires separate human review.

A literal proof of zero probability of catastrophe is not possible for
an adaptive system. Enforceable safeguards are *zero real-world actuation
authority*, bounded computation, transparent evidence and human control.

## Six-month operating cycle

The scheduled code-proposal workflow runs every ten minutes, best-effort,
between 2026-11-06 and 2027-05-06 inclusive, UTC.

1. Load the prior promoted synthetic research champion as schema-validated
   JSON data. Never execute historical candidate code.
2. Generate and mutate bounded compositions of permitted research methods;
   choose a candidate on synthetic training cases.
3. Compile a new Python implementation from a finite whitelist of
   components, and check it on 30 paired conformance trials.
4. Independently evaluate on 1,000 new synthetic cases (five seed blocks,
   five regimes), with 12 objective evaluations per method per case.
5. Compare against weighted local-search, maximin local-search and the
   existing champion (if one exists).
6. Require more than five percentage points improvement with paired 95%
   confidence lower bound above zero against every comparator; no
   regression in any simulated regime; measured compute time at most
   1.5 times each comparator; no infeasibility or false Pareto claims.
7. Promote ONLY the validated research JSON recipe if every gate passes.
   Otherwise preserve the previous champion unchanged.
8. Archive executable proposal, test evidence, acceptance/rejection,
   and the retained champion. No automatic merge or deployment.

The GitHub workflow has read-only contents and actions permissions.
Its generated source uses whitelisted operators. There is no external
model API, arbitrary downloaded code, model training, or production
access. Verification uses a stripped subprocess environment, but GitHub
Actions is not a formally verified isolation boundary.

## The ethical meaning of positive results

A Pareto gain merely means no negative gains among the three modeled
synthetic stakeholders. Unknown people, life, ecosystems and future
effects remain unmodeled. Real-world applicability is always BLOCKED
until a separate full impact analysis and explicit human approval.
Neither synthetic success nor unit tests constitute scientific discovery.

## Other boundaries and kill switch

- No source or workflow modifications by the research worker.
- No permission changes, secrets, deployment, payments or communications.
- Research compute and job runtime are capped. GitHub schedule is
  best-effort and may omit runs; account-specific billing can vary.
- A human can stop the workflow from the GitHub Actions interface.
- A rejected candidate never replaces a promoted champion.
- On absent/corrupted artifacts, restart without a champion rather
  than trusting unsafe state.
- The separate baseline workflow stops its experiments after
  2026-12-05; this six-month loop continues independently.
- The daily ChatGPT research automation can investigate broader
  human-readable source patches and propose draft pull requests, but
  no autonomous merges. Its availability and capability are conditional
  on connected tools.

## Honest remaining limitations

This is bounded algorithm composition with automatic evidence-based
selection, not general autonomous invention of algorithms or proof of
universal benefit. There is no mathematical guarantee of zero damage
for any deployment, and deployment is not authorized here.
